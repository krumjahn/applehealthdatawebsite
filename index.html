Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this a ChatGPT Health alternative?

A: Yes. It is a local-first alternative where exports and analysis can stay on your devices. You decide if and when any data is sent to external AI services.

Q: How is this different from ChatGPT Health and Claude Health?

A: The main differences are data location and portability. Cloud tools process on vendor infrastructure, while this workflow supports local processing. Exports are reusable across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and local LLMs, so your analysis stack stays modular.

Q: Can I use this with ChatGPT or Claude?

A: Yes. Exports are generated in standard formats (CSV, JSON, XML) and can be used with ChatGPT, Claude, or other assistants. You can also run local LLM analysis on Mac when you need a no-cloud path.

Q: How do I export Apple Health data from my iPhone?

A: Our iOS app, "Health Data Export," allows you to effortlessly export all your Apple Health data directly from your iPhone. Simply select your desired date range and format (CSV, JSON, or XML), and the app handles the rest securely on-device.

Q: Can I get an Apple Health CSV export?

A: Yes! The iOS app supports direct Apple Health CSV export. This format is ideal for analysis in spreadsheets like Excel or Google Sheets, offering a clear, tabular view of your health metrics.

Q: How can I analyze Apple Health data on Mac with AI?

A: After exporting your data using our iOS app, you can seamlessly transfer it to your Mac. Our accompanying Python tool (AI Analyzer) processes this data, providing AI-powered insights, visualizations, and integrations with tools like ChatGPT for advanced analysis.

Q: Is my Apple Health data secure during export and analysis?

A: Absolutely. Our iOS app performs all data processing locally on your device, ensuring your sensitive health information never leaves your iPhone or goes to the cloud. The Mac AI Analyzer also operates locally, prioritizing your privacy and data security.

Q: What is the benefit of converting Apple Health XML to CSV?

A: While XML provides a comprehensive, structured dataset, CSV is often preferred for its simplicity and compatibility with most data analysis tools, including spreadsheets. Our Mac AI Analyzer can help you easily convert Apple Health XML to CSV for more straightforward manipulation and visualization.

Q: Is this tool suitable for Apple Health data backup?

A: Yes, it's an excellent solution for comprehensive Apple Health data backup. Exporting your data to CSV, JSON, or XML gives you a personal, accessible copy of your entire health history, independent of Apple's ecosystem, for long-term safekeeping.
