Good to know

The practical questions.

Does this connect directly to my ChatGPT account?

You create a file in Health Data, then attach it to your AI conversation yourself. This gives you control over what you share. File support and size limits depend on your AI provider and plan.

Will my health data leave my iPhone?

Exports are prepared locally. On-device answers stay on your iPhone. When you share a file or choose cloud AI or cloud sync, the selected data goes to that destination. Read the privacy policy.

Apple Health already exports data. Why buy this?

Apple’s built-in export gives you an XML archive. Health Data adds CSV and JSON exports, AI summary files, on-device answers, and a Mac analysis workflow. If you only need to convert an existing XML file, try the free browser converter.

Do I need a Mac or a Pro subscription?

You can export files and use on-device answers on iPhone with the base app. Mac analysis is an additional path using the separately installed Mac app. Pro is optional for built-in cloud AI and sync workflows.
