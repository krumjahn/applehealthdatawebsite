# Pricing — Health Data Export & AI Analyzer

Last updated: 2026-08-06

AppleHealthData.com offers free local web tools, open-source developer tools, and a paid Mac app for private Apple Health export and AI analysis workflows.

## Free web tools

- Price: $0
- Billing: No account, no subscription
- Best for: Converting an Apple Health `export.xml` file into CSV or JSON in the browser
- Main URL: https://applehealthdata.com/convert-tool/
- Privacy: Runs locally in the browser; do not upload the full export to a cloud AI assistant unless you intentionally choose cloud processing
- Included tools:
  - Apple Health XML to CSV/JSON converter
  - Apple Health sleep report
  - Apple Health duplicate steps checker
  - Apple Health export splitter
  - Apple Health anonymizer

## Open-source AI/developer tools

- Price: $0
- Billing: Free open-source tools; users pay their own AI/API provider costs if they connect cloud models
- Best for: Local-first Apple Health analysis with command-line tools, local APIs, and AI agents
- Main URLs:
  - https://applehealthdata.com/apple-health-cli/
  - https://applehealthdata.com/apple-health-mcp/
  - https://applehealthdata.com/docs/for-agents.html
- Included tools:
  - `healthai` CLI for local Apple Health export analysis
  - `health-analyzer-mcp` server for Claude Desktop, Cursor, OpenClaw, and other MCP clients
  - Local REST API docs and OpenAPI specs

## Health Data Export & AI Analyzer Mac app

- Price: Paid Mac app; current price is shown on the App Store listing
- Billing: App Store purchase and any App Store-managed in-app purchases shown by Apple at checkout
- Best for: Exporting Apple Health data from iPhone, syncing to Mac, local trend analysis, dashboards, and compact summaries for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, or Copilot
- App URL: https://applehealthdata.com/go/website.html
- Product pages:
  - https://applehealthdata.com/
  - https://applehealthdata.com/apple-health-ai/
  - https://applehealthdata.com/ai-health-analysis/
- Privacy: Local-first analysis; users choose what leaves their device

## Important limitations

- AppleHealthData.com is not a medical diagnosis service.
- AI output should be treated as informational and reviewed with a qualified clinician for medical decisions.
- Pricing can vary by country, Apple App Store storefront, taxes, promotions, and App Store-managed offers.
