Where we lose

Health Data Export is the wrong pick if your week looks like Home Assistant, MQTT, Grafana, or a REST endpoint that should keep receiving Apple Health data. That is Health Auto Export’s product. Premium there is the tier for background export and sync to their Mac app. Their help center, last updated August 23, 2026, also lists auto-export to REST APIs, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, MQTT, Home Assistant, Calendar, and email.

Two limits, from their own FAQ, belong in the decision. Automations cannot read Apple Health while the iPhone is locked, and iOS chooses when background work actually runs. “Unattended” still means the phone has to be unlocked. The same Apple rule applies to any app that reads Health data, including ours.

A May 4, 2026 App Store release note for our app says REST API and Home Assistant delivery were added. The current listing’s feature summary does not sell the app that way. If REST, MQTT, Home Assistant, or Grafana is the job, stay with Health Auto Export.

Local workflow if you do not want the automation stack

  1. Export CSV or JSON from Health Data Export for a spreadsheet or a script, or export XML when the next stop is the Mac app.
  2. For a file you already exported from Apple’s Health app, convert export.xml in the browser. That converter stays on your device.
  3. On a Mac, import the XML into Health Data AI Analyzer and read the trends there before you send anything out.
  4. If you want an outside model, share the JSON summary or a short slice of the CSV. Leave the full export on the device unless you mean to upload it.
