Where we lose

This is an offline-first analysis workflow, not a medical diagnosis service and not a promise that every AI step is offline. If you use a cloud AI assistant, that assistant may process what you paste or upload. The safer workflow is to create a local summary first and only share the minimum needed for the question.

The offline Apple Health workflow

  1. Export Apple Health from iPhone, or use Direct to Mac sync when you want a no-cloud transfer.
  2. Convert export.xml to CSV, JSON, or Excel in the browser if you already have Apple's raw export.
  3. Review sleep, steps, workouts, heart rate, HRV, and other trends locally on your Mac before involving any external AI.
  4. Create a small summary file or paste only the relevant rows into your AI assistant if you want explanation or research context.
